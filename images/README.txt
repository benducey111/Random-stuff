How to add the pictures from your old site (dalhia.netlify.app):
  1. Open the old site in your browser.
  2. Right-click each picture > "Save image as..." and save it into THIS folder.
  3. Rename them photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, etc. (or any names you like).
  4. If you use different names, just update the PHOTOS list at the top of script.js.

Tip: any photo that isn't here yet shows a pretty 🌺 placeholder instead of a
broken image, so the site always looks nice while you fill it in.
