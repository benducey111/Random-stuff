💖 FOR DALHIA — how to put this site online 💖

You only have to do 3 little things, then drag-and-drop like last time.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 1 — Add the photos
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Open your OLD site folder, go into its "images" folder, and copy ALL the
pictures into the "images" folder inside THIS folder.
(They already have the right names — IMG_9375.JPG, etc. — so they'll just work.)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2 — Add the song
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Put an mp3 of "Indigo" by Sam Barber into the "music" folder, and name it
exactly:   indigo.mp3
(The music starts on the first tap — phones block auto-play, that's normal.)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 3 — Put it online
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Go to  https://app.netlify.com/drop
and DRAG this whole folder onto the page. Done! 🎉
(Or, on your existing site in Netlify: Deploys tab → drag the folder to redeploy.)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Want to change anything?
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• The exact date you got together is in script.js (START_DATE) — already set to
  July 9, 2025 so the counter reads ~10 months 25 days.
• The photo captions are in script.js (PHOTOS).
• Your message to her is in index.html (the "hero-message" part).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Dalhia can add her own photos too!
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
There's an "Add your own" section on the page where she can tap to choose
pictures (or drag them in). Those photos save right in her phone/browser, so
they stay there next time she opens the site. (They live on her device only.)
